About the company

A drilling team combining entrepreneurial and engineering expertise

We combine hands-on drilling experience, engineering and operational expertise, and an entrepreneurial approach to execute technically demanding projects — from mobilization to final reporting.

The company specializes in diamond core drilling, subcontract drilling and full-cycle support for exploration programmes.
